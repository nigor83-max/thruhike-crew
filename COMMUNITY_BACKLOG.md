# Community backlog

**There are no open community tasks in this repository.** The topics below are areas that could be considered after an owner approves a task scope and safe test boundary; they are not assignments, commitments, or invitations to begin work.

## Possible areas to consider

- Documentation and translation
- Usability and accessibility feedback
- Route and content quality
- Testing and product ideas
- Design and engineering
- Community support

Each item requires an owner, scope, acceptance criteria, safe test boundary, and publication approval before it can become an open task. No task is currently designated as beginner-friendly, available, or actively maintained.

Participation is intended to be free and accessible to newcomers. No work schedule, payment, income, equity, or ownership is promised. Any paid work would require a separate budget and written agreement before work begins.
